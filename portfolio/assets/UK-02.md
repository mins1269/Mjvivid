## Lease terms

### Residential: assured periodic tenancies (post Renters' Rights Act 2025)

The regulatory facts below carry `[SRC]` tags. Firm and asset practice is `[ASSUMED]`.
- **Tenancy type.** The Renters' Rights Act 2025 received Royal Assent on 27 Oct 2025. From **1 May 2026** all assured shorthold tenancies became assured tenancies, and fixed terms automatically became monthly periodic tenancies `[SRC: https://england.shelter.org.uk/professional_resources/legal/renting/changes_in_force]` `[SRC: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/renters-rights-act-2025-implementation-roadmap/implementing-the-renters-rights-act-2025-our-roadmap-for-reforming-the-private-rented-sector]`. All Corvel Yard tenancies are now monthly periodic. New lets since 1 May 2026 were granted as periodic from the outset.
- **Rent increases.** From 1 May 2026, s13 (Housing Act 1988) is the only lawful route. Contractual rent review clauses no longer work. The landlord must serve a notice in the prescribed form giving at least **two months'** notice, with the new rent starting on a rent date at least two months after service. Rent may rise only **once in any 12-month period**. The tenant can challenge the increase at the First-tier Tribunal, which can set the rent at no more than open-market rent (the lower of market rent and the landlord's proposal) `[SRC: https://england.shelter.org.uk/professional_resources/legal/costs_of_renting/rents_and_rent_increases/rent_increases_for_assured_tenants/]` `[SRC: https://www.parkersproperties.co.uk/guides/landlord/rent-increases-under-the-renters-rights-act-the-new-section-13-process-and-how-to-get-it-right-first-time/]`. The prescribed form number was not confirmed from a primary source (see Data gaps). The review clauses in the legacy ASTs (annual CPI+1% cap) are therefore **unenforceable** `[ASSUMED: legacy clause wording is fictional]`.
- **Rent in advance.** From 1 May 2026, landlords may not require, accept or invite more than **one month's rent in advance** `[SRC: https://www.fraser.uk.com/news/renters-rights-act-what-the-2026-rent-in-advance-ban-means-for-landlords]` `[SRC: https://hamptons.co.uk/guides/tenants/renters-rights-act-and-upfront-rent-caps-whats-changed]`.
- **Deposits.** The Tenant Fees Act 2019 cap still applies: **5 weeks' rent** where annual rent is below £50,000 (6 weeks at £50,000 or more), and a holding deposit of no more than **1 week's rent** `[SRC: https://hamptons.co.uk/guides/tenants/renters-rights-act-and-upfront-rent-caps-whats-changed]` `[SRC: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/renting-out-your-property-guidance-for-landlords-and-letting-agents/rent-payments-and-deposits]`. Corvel Yard takes 5 weeks `[ASSUMED]`.
- **Tenant notice to quit.** Two months `[ASSUMED: reported in secondary landlord guides; not verified against a primary source in this pack, see Data gaps]`.
- **Possession.** No-fault (s21) possession is abolished. Possession is only on statutory grounds. Ground details (for example the rent-arrears threshold) were not sourced in this pack. Analyst to research before the arrears strategy.
- **Repairs and standards.** The landlord is responsible for repair under statute. All units are all-electric with no gas `[ASSUMED]`. EICR last done Jun 2022, next due **Jun 2027** `[ASSUMED: 5-yearly EICR interval under the Electrical Safety Standards Regulations 2020; not web-verified]`.
- **Pets and other tenant requests.** Pet requests are handled under the Act's consent regime `[ASSUMED: detail not sourced in this pack]`.
