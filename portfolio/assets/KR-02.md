## Lease terms

**General (all leases)** [ASSUMED unless stated]
- Rent and CAM monthly in advance plus 10% VAT. CAM (관리비) covers site security, common-area cleaning, landscaping, fire-safety system maintenance, building insurance share and PM fee; recovered at cost, reconciled annually each March. Tenants pay their own electricity, water and refrigeration plant power by direct meter; for cold tenants refrigeration electricity is the largest occupancy cost and sits outside CAM [ASSUMED: market practice; no published per-평 logistics CAM benchmark found — see Data gaps].
- Deposit (보증금): 10 × initial monthly rent, fixed at signing, non-interest-bearing [ASSUMED: same convention as offices per brief; logistics deposits in practice vary and are often lower].
- Statutory overlay: 상가건물 임대차보호법 Art. 10 renewal request right (6 to 1 month before expiry, 10-year total term) may apply to warehouse leases used for business with 사업자등록; applicability to each lease: Analyst to confirm [SRC: Source 9].
- Restoration (원상복구): tenant removes racking, mezzanines, conveyors and tenant-installed MHE, repairs floor damage and dock levellers; cold tenants must return chambers at the specified temperature range with insulation panels intact [ASSUMED].
