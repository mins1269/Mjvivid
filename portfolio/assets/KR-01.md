## Lease terms

**General (all four leases)** [ASSUMED unless stated]
- Rent and CAM payable monthly in advance, plus 10% VAT. CAM (관리비) is set by the 관리단 and passed through at cost; the strata owner (the REF) bears CAM only on vacant area. CAM covers common-area cleaning, security, lifts, HVAC common plant, building insurance share and PM fee; tenants pay their own in-unit electricity by sub-meter. CAM rate reviewed each 1 January by 관리단 resolution [ASSUMED: market practice; no published GBD CAM benchmark found — see Data gaps].
- Deposit (보증금): approximately 10 × the initial monthly rent, fixed at signing and not topped up on escalation; non-interest-bearing; refundable on expiry net of arrears and restoration costs [ASSUMED: Korean office convention per brief].
- Statutory overlay: 상가건물 임대차보호법 Art. 10 — tenant may request renewal between 6 months and 1 month before expiry, within a 10-year total term; landlord may refuse only on statutory grounds. The 5% cap on rent increases does not apply where 환산보증금 exceeds the Seoul threshold of KRW 900,000,000 [SRC: Source 10]. Whether each tenant is above the threshold: Analyst to compute (환산보증금 = deposit + monthly rent × 100).
- Restoration (원상복구): tenant must reinstate to the handover condition (base-build ceiling, raised floor, carpet tile, standard lighting) at its cost before expiry; landlord may deduct from deposit [ASSUMED].
